# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is this the official Nethercap repository?

No. This is a documentation companion maintained by SCERA Official. The upstream project is [Cancro29/NETHERCAP](https://github.com/Cancro29/NETHERCAP).

## Does this repository contain the firmware?

No. The supplied firmware archive was inventoried by filename, but its contents have not been independently analysed, and redistribution permission has not been established.

## Why are the firmware and executable not included?

The firmware is a third-party dual-use tool with capabilities that may disrupt networks or deceive users. The executable's provenance and licence are also unverified. This repository therefore publishes documentation only.

## Which hardware families are mentioned by the upstream project?

The upstream project describes ESP8266, ESP32, and BW16 / RTL8720DN variants. This repository's supplied archive names refer to ESP8266 4 MB variants.

## What do SH1106 and SSD1306 mean?

They are different OLED display controller families. The archive filenames indicate which controller variant they refer to, but do not prove that a particular board or display module is compatible.

## Can I use these notes to test my neighbour's Wi-Fi?

No. Only assess networks you own or have explicit permission to assess. Do not interrupt connections, impersonate networks, or collect credentials.

## Can I contribute a safe ESP8266 project?

Yes. Documentation, passive diagnostics, sensor dashboards, and defensive configuration guidance are welcome. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Where can I find upstream background?

See the upstream [repository](https://github.com/Cancro29/NETHERCAP), [releases](https://github.com/Cancro29/NETHERCAP/releases), and [wiki](https://github.com/Cancro29/NETHERCAP/wiki). This repository does not reproduce operational attack instructions.
